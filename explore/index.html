Guided Tours

  • Tour 1: Housing, Tourism, and Urban Transformation

    Explore how residents and artist-activists resist and adapt to the overlapping pressures of tourism, gentrification, and displacement in Lavapiés.

  • Tour 2: Community Identities

    Explore how cultural producers represent, celebrate, and contest forms of identity—across demographic, cultural, and political lines—throughout the district.

  • Tour 3: Environment and Everyday Life

    Explore how environmental conditions inform cultural production and everyday experiences of habitability and urban identity in Lavapiés.

  • Tour 4: Data Transparency and Visibility

    Explore how state and institutional data shape how the district is understood—and how residents and cultural actors produce alternative forms of knowledge.
